# Desh Vromon

## Intelligent Tourism Management Platform

Desh Vromon is an intelligent tourism management platform that integrates
destination discovery, seasonal analytics, accommodation, transportation,
personalized trip planning, budget management, and tourism services into
a unified digital platform.

## Project Objectives

- Centralize tourism information
- Provide seasonal tourism insights
- Simplify accommodation and transportation planning
- Generate personalized travel plans
- Assist users with travel budget management
- Connect travelers with tourism service providers

## Core Features

- Destination Discovery
- Seasonal Analytics
- Accommodation Management
- Transportation Management
- Personalized Trip Planning
- Budget Planning
- Activity and Tour Management
- Reviews and Ratings
- Service Provider Management
- Tourism Analytics
